Institutional quality

Institutional Quality Assurance for International Schools

Quality systems should help people run a school better, not simply generate documents for inspections.

01

Policy, practice and proof

A policy sets an expectation. Daily practice shows whether it is used. Credible evidence helps leaders evaluate the result. These three elements must align for an institutional quality system to become reliable.

02

Review governance and responsibility

Clarify who makes decisions, who implements them, what information is reviewed and how actions are recorded. Look for unclear ownership, duplicated reporting and disconnected improvement activities.

03

Build a usable evidence approach

Select a manageable evidence structure tied to the school’s actual work. Avoid collecting duplicate files or assuming that a large document archive demonstrates educational effectiveness.

04

Support ongoing self-evaluation

Use periodic internal checks, practical staff briefings and concise leadership reports. The service supports better institutional decisions; it does not perform regulatory inspection or issue formal accreditation.
