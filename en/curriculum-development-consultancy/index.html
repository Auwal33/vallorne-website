Curriculum development

Curriculum Development and Review for International Schools

A curriculum works when objectives, teaching, resources and assessment reinforce one another across grades.

01

Review alignment and coherence

Compliance with a selected standards framework is only a first step. Schools also need progression between grades, suitable content depth, balanced assessment and clear expectations for classroom delivery.

02

Scope and sequence across grades

We examine what learners are expected to know and do at each stage, identify unnecessary repetition or gaps, and develop a coherent progression that teachers can use for planning.

03

Resources, assessments and teacher support

Textbooks, digital companions, formative checks, end-of-unit tasks and teacher guidance should support the same learning outcomes. Resource review considers accuracy, age appropriateness, localization and classroom usability.

04

Context without losing rigor

Saudi and GCC contextualization can make a programme relevant while retaining its intended academic standards. Scope, deliverables and approval requirements vary by curriculum and institution.
